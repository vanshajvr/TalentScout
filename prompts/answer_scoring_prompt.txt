Question asked: {question_text}
Candidate's answer: {answer_text}
Topic: {technology}
Candidate's experience level: {experience}

Score the answer on three dimensions, each from 1 to 5:

- correctness: Is the technical content accurate? Does it reflect real
  understanding rather than surface-level terminology? For behavioral
  questions, score how genuinely and specifically they answered rather
  than giving a generic non-answer.
- reasoning: Do they show judgment — trade-offs, why they'd choose one
  approach over another, what they'd check if something broke? Or do
  they just recite a definition?
- communication: Is the answer clear and well-organized, independent of
  whether the content itself is correct?

Calibrate to the stated experience level — a junior candidate giving a
solid fundamentals-level answer should score well; don't penalize them
for not discussing advanced trade-offs a senior candidate would raise.

Output strict JSON, no extra commentary:

  "correctness": <1-5>,
  "reasoning": <1-5>,
  "communication": <1-5>,
  "justification": "<one sentence, specific to this answer>"
}}
